# Peppermint Roll!
In this short candy-themed platformer, you play as a peppermint who rolls around a level made of brownies and defeats green Gummy Kids.

This game was made in 3 hours!

[Play on Itch.io!](https://obedh.itch.io/peppermint-roll)

## Controls

|Key|Action|
|---|---|
|Left Arrow Key|Move Left|
|Right Arrow Key|Move Right|
|Spacebar|Jump|

Jump on top of an enemy to kill it, like in Mario or Sonic.
